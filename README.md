## About the Project
This project is a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis of a 12-month electronics sales dataset in the US. The main goal was to extract actionable business insights from raw data to help optimize sales and marketing strategies for the upcoming year.

The script merges 12 separate monthly CSV files into a single DataFrame, cleans the data and performs grouping and visualization to answer key business questions.

## Business Questions
Through this analysis, I answered the following strategic questions:
- What was the best month for sales? (Identified the holiday season peak to help logistics and warehouse teams prepare).
- Which city generates the highest number of orders? (Pinpointed the best markets for targeted advertising).
- What time should we display advertisements to maximize the likelihood of customer's buying products? (Analyzed hourly order distribution to find daily traffic spikes).
- What products are most often sold together? (Market Basket Analysis to find cross-selling opportunities).
- What product sold the most and why? (Correlated product price with quantity ordered).
